Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Purpose

This is a personal Google OAuth application used by the owner to connect rclone to their own Google Drive account for file management and backups. It is not a public service.

Google Drive Data

The application authorizes rclone to access files in the owner's Google Drive account. Access may include listing, uploading, downloading, and managing files as configured by the owner.

Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials are maintained in the owner's private rclone configuration, not on this website. This website does not collect Google Drive files or authentication tokens. Google Drive data is not sold or used for advertising.

Access and Revocation

The owner controls the application's access and can revoke it at any time in their Google Account security settings.
